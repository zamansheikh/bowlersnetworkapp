1. Overview

Welcome to Bowlers Network, the premier amateur bowling platform that connects bowlers worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains how BowlersNetwork.com ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, protects, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

Last Updated: January 1, 2025

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

By using Bowlers Network, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following types of personal information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, password, profile picture
  • Bowling Profile: Skill level, experience, bowling style (one-handed/two-handed), average scores, high games, achievements
  • Contact Information: Phone number (optional), social media profiles (optional)
  • Location Data: Geographic location for finding nearby tournaments and bowling centers

2.2 Bowling Performance Data

  • Game scores and statistics
  • Tournament participation and results
  • Experience points (XP) and level progression
  • Team memberships and roles
  • Equipment preferences and trade history

2.3 Social and Communication Data

  • Messages sent through our platform
  • Posts, comments, and social interactions
  • Connections with other users and professionals
  • Media uploads (photos, videos of games)

2.4 Technical Information

  • Device information (type, operating system, app version)
  • Usage analytics and app performance data
  • IP address and connection information
  • Crash reports and error logs

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Core Platform Services

  • Profile Management: Create and maintain your bowling profile
  • Tournament Registration: Register you for bowling tournaments and track participation
  • Performance Analytics: Calculate statistics, track progress, and provide insights
  • Social Features: Connect you with other bowlers and professionals
  • Team Management: Facilitate team creation, communication, and coordination

3.2 Marketplace and Commerce

  • Equipment exchange platform (buying/selling bowling equipment)
  • Perks and discount management from bowling centers and manufacturers
  • Transaction history and payment processing (via secure third-party providers)

3.3 Communication and Support

  • Send notifications about tournaments, matches, and platform updates
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Facilitate messaging between users

3.4 Platform Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Develop new features based on user behavior
  • Ensure platform security and prevent fraud

4. Information Sharing

4.1 Public Information

The following information may be publicly visible on your profile:

  • Username and profile picture
  • Bowling statistics and achievements
  • Tournament participation and results
  • Level and experience points
  • Public posts and media (if shared publicly)

4.2 Authorized Sharing

  • Tournament Organizers: Registration information for events you join
  • Team Members: Profile and performance data within your teams
  • Bowling Centers: Relevant information for league participation and perks
  • Equipment Marketplace: Contact information for verified transactions

4.3 Service Providers

We share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our platform:

  • Cloud hosting and data storage providers
  • Payment processing companies
  • Analytics and performance monitoring services
  • Communication and notification services

4.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

5. Data Security

5.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data transmission uses industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption
  • Secure Storage: Personal information is stored in encrypted databases
  • Access Controls: Strict employee access controls and authentication
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Password Protection: Strong password requirements and secure authentication

5.2 Data Breach Response

In the event of a data security incident, we will:

  • Immediately investigate and contain the incident
  • Notify affected users within 72 hours
  • Provide clear information about the incident and steps taken
  • Offer support and guidance for protecting your account

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Account Management

  • Access: View and download your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format

6.2 Privacy Controls

  • Control who can view your profile and statistics
  • Manage notification preferences
  • Choose what information to share publicly
  • Block or report inappropriate users

6.3 Marketing Communications

  • Opt out of promotional emails and notifications
  • Customize communication preferences
  • Unsubscribe from marketing materials

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services:

  • Active Accounts: Information retained while account is active
  • Inactive Accounts: Data may be deleted after 2 years of inactivity
  • Tournament Records: Historical tournament data retained for 5 years for statistical purposes
  • Communication Records: Messages and posts deleted after account closure
  • Legal Requirements: Some data retained longer if required by law

8. Children's Privacy

Bowlers Network is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete such information immediately.

8.1 Teen Safety (Ages 13-17)

  • Additional privacy protections for teen users
  • Limited data collection and sharing
  • Enhanced parental notification requirements
  • Stricter communication and interaction controls

9. International Data Transfers

Bowlers Network operates globally, and your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure adequate protection through:

  • Standard contractual clauses for international transfers
  • Compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations
  • Data processing agreements with international partners
  • Regular compliance audits and assessments

10. Privacy Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will:

  • Notify users of significant changes via email or app notification
  • Post the updated policy with a new effective date
  • Provide a summary of key changes
  • Give users time to review before changes take effect

Your continued use of Bowlers Network after policy updates constitutes acceptance of the changes.
