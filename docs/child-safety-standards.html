🎳 Child Safety Standards

BowlersNetwork Commitment to Child Protection

Last Updated: October 28, 2025

Overview

BowlersNetwork is committed to providing a safe, secure, and appropriate environment for all users, particularly minors. This Child Safety Standards document outlines our comprehensive approach to protecting children from harmful content, predatory behavior, and inappropriate material. We comply with all applicable child protection laws and regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Child Safety standards.

✓ BowlersNetwork complies with all relevant child safety laws and reports to regional and national authorities as required.

1. Core Safety Features

1.1 Age Verification and Parental Controls

  • Age Gate: All users must be at least 13 years old to create an account. Users under 18 must have parental consent.
  • Account Restrictions: Minors have restricted access to certain features and messaging functionalities.
  • Parental Notification: When a minor creates an account, we collect parental information for transparency.
  • Restricted Content Access: Users under 18 cannot view, create, or interact with adult-oriented content.

1.2 Content Moderation

  • Automated Filtering: Our system uses machine learning to automatically detect and remove inappropriate content including:
    • Explicit sexual content
    • Violence and graphic images
    • Hate speech and discrimination
    • Self-harm and eating disorders content
    • Substance abuse promotion
  • Human Review: Flagged content is manually reviewed by trained moderators within 24 hours.
  • Real-time Detection: Suspicious keywords and phrases trigger automatic alerts for immediate action.

1.3 User Authentication and Account Security

  • Two-factor authentication (2FA) available for all accounts
  • Secure password requirements enforced
  • Session management and automatic logout after inactivity
  • Account verification through email confirmation
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing

2. Predator Prevention Measures

2.1 User Monitoring and Detection

  • Behavioral Analysis: We monitor user behavior patterns to detect potential predatory activity such as:
    • Inappropriate contact with minors
    • Grooming behavior patterns
    • Solicitation of personal information
    • Sexual content sharing attempts
  • Automated Alerts: Suspicious interactions trigger immediate alerts to our safety team.
  • Profile Verification: Profile information is verified against known databases of offenders.

2.2 Messaging Restrictions for Minors

  • Minors cannot send direct messages to adults outside their immediate network
  • All messages involving minors are subject to content filtering
  • Message reports are prioritized and reviewed immediately
  • Minors receive safety tips and warning signs of inappropriate contact

2.3 Profile Protection for Minors

  • Minor profiles are private by default
  • Only approved friends can view profile information
  • Location data is not shared or displayed
  • Phone numbers and personal identifiers are not visible to strangers

3. Community Reporting and Enforcement

🚨 In-App Reporting System

BowlersNetwork provides an easy-to-use in-app reporting system that allows any user to report:

  • Inappropriate content
  • Predatory behavior
  • Harassment and bullying
  • Spam and scams
  • Child safety concerns

Reports are reviewed within 24 hours and appropriate action is taken immediately.

3.1 Enforcement Actions

  • Warning System: First-time minor violations result in account warnings and education
  • Temporary Suspension: Repeated violations result in temporary account suspension
  • Permanent Ban: Serious violations including predatory behavior result in permanent account termination
  • Law Enforcement Notification: Suspected child exploitation is immediately reported to NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children) and law enforcement
  • Device Banning: Repeat offenders may be banned from accessing the platform across all devices

3.2 User Rights and Appeals

  • Users have the right to appeal account actions
  • Appeals are reviewed by a separate team within 7 days
  • Users receive clear explanation for any enforcement actions
  • Transparency is maintained throughout the process

4. Data Privacy and Protection

4.1 Data Collection

  • Minimal Collection: We collect only data necessary to provide the service
  • Parental Consent: For users under 13, verifiable parental consent is required before data collection
  • Transparency: Users and parents receive clear notification of what data is collected
  • Purpose Limitation: Data is used only for stated purposes

4.2 Data Security

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive communications
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • TLS 1.3 for data in transit
  • Regular security updates and patches
  • Secure deletion of data upon user request
  • Compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations

4.3 Data Retention

  • Personal data is retained only as long as necessary
  • User can request data deletion at any time
  • Deleted accounts are purged within 30 days
  • Backup data is securely deleted after retention period

5. Educational Resources

5.1 Safety Tips for Users

  • Keep your password private and change it regularly
  • Do not share personal information like address or phone number
  • Report suspicious behavior immediately
  • Block users who make you uncomfortable
  • Talk to a trusted adult if you experience harassment
  • Never meet someone in person that you only know online

5.2 Resources for Parents

  • Monitor your child's online activity and connections
  • Have regular conversations about online safety
  • Use privacy settings to restrict contact from strangers
  • Report concerns to BowlersNetwork and local authorities
  • Contact the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) for additional resources

5.3 Links to External Resources

  • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)
  • Common Sense Media - Digital Safety
  • Federal Trade Commission - Child Safety
  • Internet Watch Foundation

6. Child Safety Concerns - Reporting

🚨 If You Encounter Child Exploitation

If you witness or suspect child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child exploitation:

  1. Report it immediately through the in-app report feature
  2. Report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) at CyberTipline.org
  3. Contact local law enforcement
  4. Do not attempt to contact the suspected offender

6.1 Law Enforcement Cooperation

  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations
  • We retain logs and data as required by law
  • We respond to lawful requests within 48 hours
  • We have designated contacts for emergency law enforcement requests

6.2 NCMEC and CyberTipline

We are committed to supporting the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and maintain compliance with the CyberTipline requirements. All suspected child exploitation is reported to NCMEC as required by law.

7. User Content Policies

7.1 Prohibited Content

  • Sexual Content: No sexually explicit, suggestive, or nude content. Violators face immediate permanent ban.
  • Violence: No graphic violence, gore, or content promoting violence. Warnings issued, escalating to ban.
  • Hate Speech: No content targeting individuals or groups based on protected characteristics. Immediate action taken.
  • Harassment: No bullying, threats, or targeted harassment. Reported users are suspended and may be banned.
  • Illegal Activity: No promotion or instruction for illegal activities. Reports made to law enforcement as appropriate.
  • Self-Harm: No content promoting self-harm, eating disorders, or suicide. Crisis resources provided.
  • Drug/Alcohol Abuse: No content promoting illegal drug or alcohol abuse by minors.

7.2 Content Review Process

  • Automated systems filter prohibited content in real-time
  • Users can appeal content decisions
  • Clear guidelines provided for what violates policy
  • Educational approach for first-time violations

8. Compliance and Certifications

  • COPPA Compliant: Full compliance with Children's Online Privacy Protection Act
  • GDPR Compliant: General Data Protection Regulation compliance for EU users
  • CCPA Compliant: California Consumer Privacy Act compliance
  • Google Play Safety Standards: Full compliance with Google Play app safety requirements
  • Apple App Store Guidelines: Full compliance with Apple App Store guidelines
  • NCMEC Cooperation: Active participant in NCMEC CyberTipline program

Users will be notified at least 30 days before any material changes take effect. Continued use of the app following notification of changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
